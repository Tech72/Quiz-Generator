Reading Speed

Learn From My Quizzes
Each first go at a quiz measures how much longer you take on longer questions and moves the speed below towards it. Retakes, redo rounds, maths questions, wrong answers and questions you went back to aren't used.
Answer Reading (wpm):
How fast you read the answer choices. Answers of about 50 words have a target to answer in: the share of your gold pace that answering took this round (the whole gold pace for a question of about 30 words when the answers aren't held back). Once this speed is known, longer ones get extra time to read at it before they count as slow.With learning on, changing it sets where learning carries on from.
Question Reading (wpm):
How fast you read the question itself. With Show Answers After Reading on, the answers appear on their own after the question's length at this speed, adjusted to how you're reading the current quiz. Fill in the blank uses it for question length when a quiz can't show how your time grows with length.With learning on, it's measured whenever you press to show the answers on a first go.

    B. @@Mars. [explanation] Mars is often called the Red Planet due to its iron oxide-rich surface.@@
    C. Jupiter.
    D. Earth.
